FocusTrack Privacy Policy

App: FocusTrack (com.dwivedi.focustrack) · Developer: Aditya Dwivedi · Effective: 20 November 2026

Summary

FocusTrack measures your screen time and helps you limit it. All data stays on your device. FocusTrack has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

What the app accesses

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): with your permission, FocusTrack reads Android's usage statistics: which apps you used, for how long, how many times you opened them, and how many times you unlocked your phone.
  • Data you create: the limits, schedules, focus sessions and settings you set up in the app.
  • Notifications: used only to show limit warnings, focus-session status and an optional daily summary.

Accessibility service

FocusTrack includes an optional app blocker built on Android's AccessibilityService API. It is off until you turn it on, and the app explains what it does before you do. When enabled, it only receives the name of the app that comes to the foreground, so it can show a block screen for apps you chose to restrict. It cannot read screen content (window content retrieval is disabled in its configuration), and it does not record, store, or share anything it observes.

Where data is stored

In a private database inside the app's storage on your device. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Sharing

FocusTrack does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal or device data with the developer or any third party. Backup files and CSV reports are only created when you ask, and you choose where to save them. Anything you do with those files afterwards is up to you.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete everything in Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. You can revoke usage access, notifications or the accessibility service at any time in Android Settings.

Children

FocusTrack is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and shipped inside the app, with a new effective date.
